## Participation

Class participation is 2% of the final grade. Participation includes questions, responses, short activities, discussion, and relevant use of the Zoom chat.

Please turn on your camera when circumstances permit. Cameras support more direct discussion in an online class. Camera use is encouraged, but the participation grade is not based only on camera availability.

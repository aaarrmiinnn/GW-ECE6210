# Mid-semester project presentation

## Overview

Each team gives a short presentation on its group project.
The goal is to share your project idea and get feedback early.

You do not need to go into detail.
Results are not expected at this point.
You do not need to choose a specific learning problem or algorithm yet.
A clear idea of the problem and the data is enough.

- **Date:** October 14, during the regular class meeting on Zoom
- **Length:** 10 minutes per team
- **Slides:** 5 to 10 slides, not counting the title slide
- **Grade:** Not graded. The presentation is part of the final project package.

## Time

Plan for about 8 minutes of talk and 2 minutes of questions.
We will stop you at 10 minutes so that every team has its full time.
Practice at least once with a timer.

Every team member must present.
Split the slides so that each person speaks for about the same amount of time.

## What to include

Your slides should cover the items below.
Keep each item short.
You can combine items on one slide.

### 1. Title slide

- Project title
- Team member names
- One sentence that states the project

### 2. Engineering problem

- What engineering problem are you solving?
- Why does it matter, and who would use the result?

### 3. Data

- Where does the data come from? Give the dataset name and link, or describe the simulation.
- About how many samples and features are there?
- If you have the data, show one plot of it.

### 4. Measuring success

- How will you know the project worked?
- What will you measure? Examples: error, accuracy, false alarms, or missed detections.

### 5. Computation, memory, or latency

- What resource limit matters for your problem?
- How might you measure it? Examples: model size, number of parameters, or inference time.

### AI use disclosure

If you used an AI tool for any part of the project or the slides, add a short note on the last slide.
Name the tool and say briefly how you used it.
See the AI use policy in the syllabus.

## Submission

- Upload your slides as one PDF file to Blackboard before class on the day of the presentation.
- Name the file `ECE6210_Midterm_<LastNames>.pdf`, using the last names of all team members.
- Keep the file. It is part of your final project package.
- Share your own screen on Zoom during the talk.
- Test screen sharing and your microphone before class.

## Slide tips

- Do not put code in your slides.
- Use one main idea per slide.
- Use large fonts. Others will view your slides on small screens.
- Label every plot axis and include units.
- Prefer plots and tables over long text.
- Number your slides so that others can refer to them in questions.
